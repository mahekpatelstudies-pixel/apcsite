About the Campaign

What is Beyond Limits?

Beyond Limits is a cross-campus awareness campaign organised by the University of Europe for Applied Sciences in partnership with the Asian Paralympic Committee. Running September to October 2026, the campaign brings the Paralympic spirit directly onto campus through participatory activities that give students a direct, personal encounter with Paralympic sport.

This first activation centres on one flagship sporting experience and one creative competition, executed with full focus. Additional sporting experiences are being developed as part of a future Beyond Limits activation series.

The Activations

🏐  On Campus · Para Sport

Beyond Limits Sitting Volleyball Challenge

30 September 2026

Students play sitting volleyball on a lowered net in teams of six, maintaining floor contact throughout. Movement is restricted entirely to the upper body, changing the pace and strategy of the game completely. It is more physically demanding than it looks and levels the playing field across all athletic abilities. No prior experience is required.

🎨  Online · Creative

Human Spirit — Art and Story Competition

25 September — 5 October 2026

An online creative competition open to students across all partner universities simultaneously. Students submit original artwork and a written piece of 150 to 300 words about a real person who has shown extraordinary determination in the face of adversity or limitation. Judged by a panel from UE, APC, and the student body. Winners receive official certificates signed by both UE and APC. A permanent digital gallery is created from all qualifying entries.

Future Ambassador Programme

One seat. One campus.
Is it yours?

The Future Ambassador Programme selects one student per participating university to serve as the official campus voice of Beyond Limits. Ambassadors lead on-campus engagement, recruit a small team of fellow students, post consistently on social media, and are formally recognised at the close of the campaign.
